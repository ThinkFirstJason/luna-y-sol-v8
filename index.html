Welcome

Luna y Sol is a small, boutique studio on Colorado Boulevard, built around curly and wavy hair. Most salons shy away from texture. We specialize in it.

Sit down, exhale, and tell us about your hair. We'll take it from there.

Services

From dry-cut curly transformations to lived-in balayage, everything we do is tailored to your texture.

Curly & Wavy Cuts

Our specialty

The cornerstone of everything we do. Using the dry-cutting method, we sculpt each cut to the unique character of your curls, maximizing spring, minimizing bulk, and giving you a shape that wears beautifully on day one and day five.
